PRIVACY

Your information deserves care.

Here’s what information Visual Risk handles, why it is used, and the choices you have when using the service.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This notice describes how Visual Risk and AI Intelligence (“Visual Risk” or “the service”) handles information submitted through the application. It applies to use of the service’s account, dashboard, and personal planning features.

1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, the service may handle:

  • Account details such as your username, email address, and password credential (stored as a password hash).
  • Profile information such as age, occupation, income, expenses, savings, and study, fitness, or sleep information.
  • Entries you create, including transactions, study and fitness logs, and goals.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and manage your account, display the records and dashboards you request, calculate summaries and estimates, and operate and protect the service. Do not enter information that is not needed for these purposes.

3. Storage and security

Application records are stored by the service in its configured database. Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information, but no online service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your password and sign out on shared devices.

4. Cookies and local storage

The application uses a session cookie to support sign-in and may use your browser’s local storage to remember the display theme. These technologies support basic service functionality and preferences.

5. Sharing and third-party services

Information may be accessible to the people who operate and maintain the service as needed to provide it. The application’s pages may load libraries or assets from third-party content delivery networks; your browser may connect to those providers when loading a page. Do not assume that information is protected from the service operator or its hosting environment.

6. Retention and your choices

Information is retained in the application database for as long as it remains there. The application may not provide a self-service way to export or delete your account or records. For a request about your information, contact the operator through the channel by which you access the service. Applicable legal rights may vary by location.

7. Children’s privacy

The service is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the service operator so the situation can be reviewed.

8. Updates to this notice

This notice may change as the service or its practices change. Any revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
